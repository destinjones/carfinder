What it does

Three prices, not one.

Car sites show you the sticker. Dealers quote you a monthly payment. The number you actually need, what leaves your bank account, is the one nobody prints. CarFinder puts all three on every card.

On every card

List · dealer · drive-off

The maker's price from base trim to top, the band dealers really sell that model in this month (at sticker, a little under, or deep discounts), and the estimated total with tax, title and fees for your state.

Your state or city

Local taxes and fees

Pick a US state, an Indian state, a Chinese city, an Australian state, a Mexican estado, a Spanish region, a Russian region, a Brazilian state or a Canadian province, and every price on the page recomputes with its sales tax, road tax, plates, insurance and rebates.

Help me choose

Four questions

Budget with taxes in, what the car will mostly do, fuel, and whether you need all-wheel drive. The helper sets the filters for you; loosen any of them afterwards.

Window sticker

Every line of the cost

Open a car and drag the trim slider from base to fully loaded. The breakdown updates line by line: destination, discount, tax, doc fee, registration, insurance, grants, with a note on what each one is and whether you can negotiate it.

Running costs

Fuel, charging and the loan

Yearly fuel or charging cost at your local prices and distance, an estimated monthly payment, and the assumptions behind them, all editable. Sort by lowest yearly fuel cost or cheapest per horsepower.

Compare

Four cars side by side

Tick up to four cars and compare price, drive-off, monthly, efficiency, power, seats, drivetrain and market notes in one table, on a phone too.

Units

MPG and miles for everyone

Every figure outside the US carries its US-unit twin: 17.4 kmpl / 41 mpg, 543 km / 337 mi range, 53 mpg UK / 44 mpg US. The World tab lets you pick mpg, L/100 km or km/L outright.

World tab

Every market in one list

All 2,729 cars converted to one currency at ECB rates, so you can see what a Seagull costs against a Mirage, rank the cheapest horsepower on Earth, and jump to the home tab for the local rules.

Plain English

What the terms mean

MSRP, destination, doc fee, OTR, ex-showroom, RTO, 落地价, tenencia, IPVA, ОСАГО: each tab has a glossary written for someone buying their first car there.

How to use it

From “I need a car” to a shortlist.

Nothing to install and nothing to sign up for. Open the page, pick your country, and work down the list.

  1. Pick your country tab

    The tabs across the top switch the whole page: the cars, the price basis (MSRP, ex-showroom, OTR, 指导价, RRP…), the tax rules and the glossary. World pools every country in one list.

  2. Pick your state or city

    The picker under the search bar (next to it on a desktop) sets the taxes and fees. Every drive-off price on the page recomputes for it. The page remembers your choice.

  3. Narrow it down

    Tap the body-type and fuel chips, or open Filters for price range, size, brand tier, seats, drivetrain, minimum MPG, minimum electric range and brand. Type a make or model to search. Or press Help me choose and answer four questions.

  4. Sort by what matters to you

    Price, lowest yearly fuel cost, best MPG, longest electric range, most horsepower, cheapest per horsepower, most seats. On a phone the sort lives behind the ⇅ button next to the search box.

  5. Open a car

    Tap any card for the cost breakdown. Drag the trim slider from base to top trim and watch every line move. The Why this price band panel explains the market for that model and what to ask the dealer.

  6. Compare and decide

    Tap + Compare on up to four cards, then Compare in the bar at the bottom. Remove one, add another, and go back to the list with your filters intact.

  7. Check the terms and the fine print

    What the terms mean explains every word on the page for your country. How these numbers work at the bottom of each tab says exactly what is estimated and how.

Reading a card

What the numbers mean.

01Sticker (MSRP)
The maker's price for the cheapest trim, with the spread up to the top trim and the mandatory destination charge underneath. Outside the US the label changes with the country: ex-showroom, OTR, guide price, RRP, precio de lista, PVP, tabela.
02Est. drive-off · your state
The range you should expect to pay in total. It starts from the sticker, applies the typical dealer discount or markup for that model, then adds your state's tax, a typical doc fee and registration. Bargain within it; don't accept above it.
03Efficiency, power, seats, drive
EPA combined mpg or electric range for the US; ARAI, WLTP, CLTC, ADR or NRCan figures elsewhere, each with its mpg and miles twin. The yearly fuel estimate uses your local fuel price and distance.
04Market badge
Red: sells at or above sticker, expect a wait. Grey-blue: small discounts are typical. Green: big discounts and incentives; this is where the deals are.
05Tags
Body type and size, fuel, brand tier, plus flags for 2025 leftovers (discounted), 2027 early arrivals, new or updated models, and preliminary pricing that can still change.

How it's built

One file. No framework.

A single HTML page

Markup, CSS and plain JavaScript in one self-contained file with the data inlined, so it works from a file:// URL and keeps working offline once loaded. No build tools beyond a 60-line Node script that stitches the parts together.

Data as JSON, rules as functions

Each country's cars are a JSON file with a documented row schema. Each country's taxes and fees are one function that returns the breakdown lines, so a wrong rate is a one-line fix and a pull request.

Tested before it ships

node --test validates every row of every dataset, then runs the pricing core for all 44,000 car-and-region combinations and checks known quotes. A headless Chromium pass exercises every tab and control at phone and desktop widths.

Estimates, labelled as estimates

Dealer prices are bands by demand level, not quotes. Taxes follow the published rules on the compile date. Every tab ends with a plain-language note on what was assumed, and models with provisional pricing carry a Preliminary tag.

git clone https://github.com/destinjones/carfinder
cd carfinder
node src/build.js        # stitches src/ + data/ into carfinder.html
node --test              # schema checks, pricing core, known quotes
python3 -m http.server   # then open http://localhost:8000/

Use it as a starting point, not a quote. Prices were compiled on 29 September 2026 from manufacturer price lists and the main listing sites in each country; incentives, fuel prices and exchange rates move monthly and tax rules change with budgets. Always get the out-the-door price in writing from two or three dealers. If you spot a wrong price or rate, open an issue or fix the JSON and send a pull request.
